Application

This part of my research focuses on applying sensing and computational methods to real problems in robotics, tactile perception, localization, vibration measurement, human-centred sensing, and immersive systems. The goal is to move from sensor design and modeling toward complete systems that can perceive, estimate, interact, and act.

Texture Perception

Image placeholder: insert a texture specimen, whisker texture setup, roughness classification result, or visual-tactile dataset figure.

Texture perception studies how sensing systems can identify surface properties such as roughness and hardness. This application connects tactile sensing, surface interaction, feature extraction, and machine learning.

Whisker-like sensors can collect temporal tactile signals during sweeping or dabbing interactions. These signals can then be used to classify surface roughness, distinguish material properties, and study artificial tactile perception.

Robot Localization and Navigation

Image placeholder: insert a robot localization experiment, map interaction, whisker contact model, or feasible-state plot.

Contact-based sensing can support robot localization and navigation in situations where visual sensing is limited, unreliable, or unavailable. Whisker-like sensors provide local contact information that can help a robot infer its position relative to nearby boundaries.

This application is especially relevant for confined spaces, visually degraded environments, close-range navigation, and robots that must physically interact with their surroundings.

Mobile Robot Navigation Using Whiskers

Image placeholder: insert a mobile robot with whisker sensors or a tactile navigation experiment.

Whisker-based navigation uses tactile contact to detect nearby objects, obstacles, narrow passages, surface features, and environmental constraints. This allows mobile robots to explore through direct physical interaction.

A whisker sensor can provide a lightweight and mechanically simple way to give a robot near-field tactile awareness. This is useful for mobile robots operating in cluttered, dark, dusty, or visually complex environments.

Contact-Based Vibrometry

Image placeholder: insert a whisker-based vibrometer setup or vibration measurement result.

Contact-based vibrometry uses a lightweight whisker-like probe to measure vibration at a specific location. The aim is to collect vibration information without significantly changing the natural response of the structure being measured.

This application connects bio-inspired sensing with vibration analysis, structural monitoring, robotic inspection, and contact-based diagnostics.

Ball Catching Robotics

Image placeholder: insert a Kinect-based ball-catching setup, robotic arm, ball trajectory, or catching experiment.

Ball catching is an application of perception-driven robotic action. The system must detect a flying ball, track its motion, estimate the trajectory, predict a catching point, and move the manipulator to intercept the ball.

This application combines visual sensing, trajectory estimation, robot kinematics, reachable-space analysis, control, and human-robot interaction. It represents a complete perception-action pipeline.

Human Physiological Sensing

Image placeholder: insert a physiological sensing setup, ECG/HRV result, thermal image, or experimental protocol figure.

Human physiological sensing applies sensors and signal analysis to study bodily responses. This includes measurements such as ECG, heart-rate variability, thermal imaging, breath temperature, breath exergy, and other physiological indicators.

This research direction connects sensing, biomedical signal processing, experimental design, and human-centred interpretation.

VR and Human-Centred Perception Studies

Image placeholder: insert a VR experiment setup, user study, EEG/ERP setup, or human-centred sensing diagram.

Virtual reality provides a controlled environment for studying human perception, behaviour, motion response, sensory conflict, interaction, and experience. It allows sensory stimuli to be designed, synchronized, and measured with experimental precision.

This application area connects immersive systems, human-subject experiments, haptics, physiological sensing, EEG/ERP studies, behavioural measures, and psychological assessment.
